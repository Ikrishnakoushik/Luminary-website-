Be Respectful & Civil

Treat everyone with respect. Harassment, hate speech, and personal attacks are strictly prohibited. We cultivate a space of intellectual generosity and constructive collaboration.

Share Knowledge, Not Noise

Ensure every spread, review, and comment adds genuine value. Low-effort spam, repetitive bot promotion, and irrelevant sales links will be suppressed.

Authenticity & Integrity

Do not impersonate other authors or spread fabricated claims. When synthesizing information or referencing research, cite your origins with intellectual honesty.
